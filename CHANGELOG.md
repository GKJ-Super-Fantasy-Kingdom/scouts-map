# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Add markers for quest turn-ins, spell pickups, captured monsters, relic and item encounters, unit rewards, recruits, and hero unlocks.
- Mark pirate returns, ready Wonder rewards, Witch bonfires, hospitals, and resource trades.
- Add optional markers for rare finds, high-value salvage, and achievement-based unit cages.

## 0.1.2

- Darken the entire badge while its target is unavailable, including when an expansion is unaffordable. Keep the lock and hover reason legible.

## 0.1.1

- Distinguish event and expansion types with high-contrast illustrations and a visible hover outline.
- Keep locked markers readable and place their names and availability messages inside the HUD-safe frame.

## 0.1.0

- Add directional markers for discovered roads, visible shops, native treasure, hermits and world rewards.
- Left-click available markers to focus the camera. Show a lock and availability reason for supported blocked markers.
