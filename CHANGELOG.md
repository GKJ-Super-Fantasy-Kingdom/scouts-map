# Changelog

## 0.4.2

- Give actionable map badges a stepped white outer edge on mouse hover and press. Keep the dark inner edge and category color visible, and keep locked badges muted during hover.

## 0.4.1

- Anchor on-screen badges to the drawn sprite so padded art and oversized interaction boxes no longer leave a large gap.

## 0.4.0

- Mark open quests, restorable kingdom sites, Black Markets, Trading Posts, and additional discovered world services.
- Configure marker categories independently, including separate settings for open quests and quest turn-ins.

## 0.3.1

- Move hover tooltips to a clear side of the marker when nearby badges would be covered.

## 0.3.0

- Anchor on-screen markers above their targets with a downward arrow.
- Mark discovered monsters to provoke and show the native availability reason.
- Add distinct marker frames and use native shop, building, and encounter icons where available.

## 0.2.0

- Add markers for quest turn-ins, spell pickups, captured monsters, relic and item encounters, unit rewards, recruits, and hero unlocks.
- Mark pirate returns, ready Wonder rewards, Witch bonfires, hospitals, and resource trades.
- Add optional markers for rare finds, high-value salvage, and achievement-based unit cages.

## 0.1.2

- Darken the entire badge while its target is unavailable, including when an expansion is unaffordable. Keep the lock and hover reason legible.

## 0.1.1

- Distinguish event and expansion types with high-contrast illustrations and a visible hover outline.
- Keep locked markers readable and place their names and availability messages inside the HUD-safe frame.

## 0.1.0

- Add directional markers for discovered roads, visible shops, native treasure, hermits and world rewards.
- Left-click available markers to focus the camera. Show a lock and availability reason for supported blocked markers.
